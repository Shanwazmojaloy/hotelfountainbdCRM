## Verdict

The site was **~80% technically solid**. Six concrete gaps were found — **all fixed and deployed**. This maxes out *technical* SEO (rich-snippet eligibility + full AI-crawler access). It does **not** by itself guarantee #1 ranking — competitive local terms depend on off-site levers (reviews + GBP freshness).

## How to Verify the Results

## The Real Ranking Levers (off-site, owner-only)

Technical SEO can't move these:

1. **Review count** — peers rank on volume (White Stone / Grace 21: 86–1.1K reviews vs our 349 spread across platforms). The auto post-checkout review email (shipped 2026-07-14) is the long-game fix.
2. **GBP freshness** — photos stale ~143 days; add 15–20 fresh shots.
3. **OTA damage control** — 1-review OTA pages (MakeMyTrip 1.0/5, Agoda 2.8/10) rank on branded SERP; a handful of genuine reviews each neutralizes them.
4. **Social + Ads** — merge duplicate FB page; delete/rewrite the paused Ramadan ad.
