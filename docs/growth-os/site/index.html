Straight answers

What owners ask us first

Will my staff actually use it?

That is the real question, and it is why training is included rather than sold separately. The front desk screen was designed against our own staff — people who had used a paper register for years. If a screen needs explaining twice, we change the screen.

What happens to our existing data?

We migrate your current guest list and open reservations during setup. You keep your registers running in parallel for the first two weeks — nobody jumps without a net.

Do we need fast internet?

It runs in a normal browser on a normal broadband line. No server to buy, no software to install on each machine.

Is our guest data safe?

Each hotel's data is isolated at the database level. Staff see only what their role allows, and deletions are restricted to admin accounts and logged.

What if we want to leave?

Month to month. Give us notice and we export your data in a standard format. We would rather earn the next month than trap you into it.

Can it connect to Booking.com and Agoda?

Channel sync is on the roadmap and partly built. We will tell you exactly what is live today rather than promise it in a demo — ask during the call.
